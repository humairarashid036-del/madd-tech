A clear delivery path

From first conversation
to structured handover.

Each engagement is shaped around agreed scope, milestones, communication, dependencies and acceptance criteria.

STEP 01

Discover

Discuss product goals, design objectives, interfaces, constraints and schedule.

STEP 02

Define

Agree the work scope, deliverables, assumptions, tools and acceptance criteria.

STEP 03

Plan

Set milestones, communication channels, review points and issue-management procedures.

STEP 04

Execute

Perform the agreed work and provide progress updates and technical records.

STEP 05

Review

Review deliverables and address feedback agreed within the engagement.

STEP 06

Handover

Provide approved deliverables, documentation and a close-out summary under contract.
